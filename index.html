Tell us where customers get stuck

Good questions, straight answers.

How long does a project take?

A focused landing page can often be completed in about a week after content is agreed. Progressive web apps take longer depending on ordering, booking and admin features.

Why not just use Facebook or Instagram?

Social profiles help people discover you. Your own site gives customers one clear place to understand the offer, take action and find reliable business information.

Do I own the website or PWA?

Yes. Once the agreed project is paid for, the final files and custom design are yours.

Can you write the content?

Yes. We can turn your menu, services, notes and existing profiles into clear customer-facing copy as part of the build.

What happens after launch?

Small fixes related to the delivered build are covered for an agreed period. Larger updates and new features can be quoted separately.

How does payment work?

Payment terms are confirmed in writing before work begins, based on the final scope. You will always know what is included first.

How much does it cost?

A landing page is $149 and includes a focused responsive page, customer-facing copy shaped from your business information, final testing and the agreed launch files. A $299 progressive web app adds a guided customer flow such as booking or ordering, plus installable phone access.

What results can I expect?

A clearer self-service journey can mean fewer routine booking calls and more direct orders, depending on your audience, offer and promotion. We do not guarantee specific sales, booking or revenue results.
